# SQL--Business-Analysis-of-Target-Brand
Target is a globally renowned brand and a prominent retailer in the United States. As a data analyst I have analyze the Company data. I have provided few insights and recommendations based on my analysis.

💡 **What I Did:**

🔹 Imported, cleaned, and explored **Target Brazil’s SQL dataset** containing 100K orders from 2016–2018.

🔹 Wrote **complex SQL queries** using joins, aggregations, and date functions for trend and regional analysis.

🔹 Applied **window functions** and **CTEs** to calculate year-over-year growth, delivery time, and freight averages.

🔹 Utilized **CASE statements** and time extraction to segment orders by purchase time and payment methods.

🔹 Built **insight-driven SQL reports** covering customer distribution, sales performance, and logistics metrics.

🎯 What Has Been Done in This Project:

🔹 **Data Collection & Cleaning:** Imported and explored Target Brazil’s SQL dataset containing over 100,000 e-commerce orders from 2016–2018. Cleaned and validated data across multiple tables — customers, orders, order items, and payments — ensuring data consistency and accuracy for analysis.

🔹 **Data Modeling & Preparation:** Built structured SQL models using **joins, aggregations, and common table expressions (CTEs)** to combine key data points. Prepared datasets to calculate essential business metrics such as total sales value, freight costs, delivery time, and order volume across different states.

🔹 **Exploratory Data Analysis in SQL:** Leveraged **date extraction, CASE statements, and window functions** to uncover sales trends, identify monthly and yearly seasonality, and analyze customer behavior by time of day and region.

🔹 **Performance & Logistics Analysis:** Conducted deep analysis of **state-wise performance**, freight value, and delivery efficiency. Compared estimated vs. actual delivery timelines to detect operational delays and highlight states with exceptional logistics performance.

🔹 **Insight Generation:** Identified a **137% increase in total order cost** between 2017 and 2018, discovered top-performing customer regions (SP, RJ, MG), and provided actionable recommendations to optimize marketing strategies, enhance delivery reliability, and expand customer reach across underperforming regions.


💡 Key Takeaways from the Dashboard:

🔹 Orders spanned from **Sept 2016 to Oct 2018**, showing a strong upward trend with peak activity during **holiday months (Oct–Nov)**.

🔹 The business reached **27 states and 4,119 cities**, with **São Paulo (SP)** leading in both customers and orders, while **RR, AC, and AP** showed minimal sales.

🔹 The **total order cost increased by 137%** from 2017 to 2018 (Jan–Aug), reflecting substantial revenue growth.

🔹 **SP** generated the highest total revenue, but **PB** recorded the **highest average order value**, indicating a premium customer base.

🔹 Freight analysis revealed **SP** had the most orders, whereas **RR** incurred the **highest average freight cost per order**.

🔹 Delivery performance showed **delays in many states**, though regions like **AC, RO, and AM** achieved **faster-than-expected delivery times**.


Overall, the analysis showed strong business growth and expanding market presence across Brazil between 2016 and 2018. Orders increased steadily, peaking during holiday seasons, with São Paulo (SP) emerging as the top-performing state. Revenue rose by 137% year-over-year, though regional disparities in freight costs and delivery delays were evident. Despite logistic challenges, the data reflected solid customer engagement and growing e-commerce adoption nationwide.
